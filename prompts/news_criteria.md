Real news = a concrete, NEW event within the period under review. Test: can the core be phrased as
"On <date>, <actor> <decided / published / did something>" or "On <date>, <something> happened"?
If not, it is not news.

Typical kinds, regardless of subject area:
- Decisions: laws, rulings, elections, regulation, resolutions of authorities or bodies
- Releases: studies, data, statistics, reports, products, releases, works
- Incidents: accidents, attacks, outages, scandals, leaks, crises
- Changes: funding rounds, acquisitions, insolvencies, personnel changes, changes of strategy
- Results: figures, measurements, records, outcomes of proceedings or competitions

NOT news: opinion, essays, timeless analysis or explainers on a known topic; interviews or podcasts
without new information; guides, tutorials, reviews without a specific occasion; retrospectives on
things that happened long ago; self-promotion and announcements by the newsletter itself.
